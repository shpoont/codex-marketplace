# Evaluation workflow

Use this guidance to operate an ongoing investigation without losing its
purpose between setup, trials, repairs, and resumed work. Apply the
[shared package safeguards](../../../references/official-skill-policy.md);
load only the methodology sections relevant to the current concern.

## Working record and resumption

Keep one short current record in the project's existing evaluation or handoff
location. Update it as work changes; link detailed evidence rather than making
another reporting system. For a small one-off task, use the response itself.
Include:

- the observed problem and decision this evaluation should inform;
- the current stage and the case's success rule;
- valid evidence, its tested revisions, and unresolved findings;
- the next action, why it is necessary, and its completion condition;
- any explicit user limits, useful execution estimates and the stopping condition.

Distinguish preparation, evaluator calibration, execution-path qualification,
completed agent work, and demonstrated repair. Hundreds of component checks
do not complete an untested agent conversation. Broad authorization still
requires the operator to choose proportionate work tied to the decision;
routine steps within that authorization do not need repeated approval.

On resume, reconcile the record with retained outputs, effective inputs,
installed versions, and remaining authority and usage. Check the dependencies
relevant to the next action, preserving historical records. Reuse applicable
evidence after checking compatibility; do not repeat the whole suite merely
because the conversation resumed. Apply the
[reuse rules](result-interpretation.md#reruns-regrading-and-reuse) when inputs
or grading changed. Missing records remain unknown rather than being inferred
from a previous readiness headline.

## Establish a complete path early

Before substantial setup changes, establish one realistic case through the normal
prepare, run, collect, grade and report path. Check the actual selected agent
instructions and complete rendered grading input, not just templates or component
checks. Reuse compatible retained evidence when it proves that path; record its
source, settings and tested revisions rather than dispatching a new trial merely
to create a baseline. This proves only the covered case and conditions.

If the path is blocked, name the missing result and smallest correction needed
to reach it. Validate that correction through the affected path. Do not turn the
baseline into a general infrastructure campaign or call component success a
completed evaluation.

## Constraints and recovery

Use restrictions to meet a concrete requirement or manage a demonstrated risk;
explain what they protect and what work they prevent. An agent-proposed no-retry
rule that the user approved remains binding within its agreed scope. It is not
an enduring preference, evidence that the restriction was necessary, or authority
to impose it on later work. Keep its origin and scope visible when resuming.
Do not silently remove a still-applicable limit; ask if its scope materially
affects the next action and cannot be resolved from the record.

Choose recovery from what actually happened:

| Situation | Next action within existing authority |
| --- | --- |
| Setup, collection or grading defect | Diagnose, correct and rerun the affected check. Reuse saved target outputs when valid; preserve the original error and distinguish regrading from new agent behavior. |
| Valid agent failure | Preserve it. Retest a concrete repair claim; do not retry until a pass appears or weaken the criterion to turn failure into success. |
| Unknown operation outcome | Reconcile actual state before repeating. A missing controller reply does not establish failure or make a mutable operation safe to replay. |
| Actual explicit user limit | Honor its stated scope until changed. Diagnose and continue independent work only where that scope allows; a routine correction does not override the limit. |

Ordinary repairs and affected retests do not need another approval when already
authorized. These defaults permit neither unlimited retries nor a new approval
loop. Ask only for a genuinely missing decision or a material change to scope,
access, cost or risk, or before exceeding an actual constraint. Preserve the
grading meaning and historical evidence while simplifying implementation inside
the agreed route and boundaries. A materially different fallback still requires
agreement under the package safeguards.

## Cumulative effort and blockers

Before expanding setup, adding tooling, or continuing after repeated repairs,
reassess the whole route to the original decision. Account for cumulative
setup time, probes, model usage and unfinished dependencies, including work
that has not yet reached a real agent attempt. Passing local checks and prior
investment do not establish that another repair is worth doing.
Explain whether the route still advances the requested result and whether the
remaining benefit justifies its work and delay. Prefer the simpler sufficient
implementation; do not preserve accidental mechanics as if they were grading
requirements. Keep the original cases, criteria and results traceable.

For the next repair, name the demonstrated blocker, the evidence the repair
will unlock, the smallest relevant validation, and where to stop if it fails.
Distinguish a bounded defect within the agreed route from a route that needs
new privileges, authentication, providers, infrastructure, or substantially more
work. Stop the affected line when its required boundary is unavailable; present
the remaining path and tradeoffs for a decision rather than proposing one
small change at a time without the cumulative picture. Do not weaken a
safeguard to make a stalled evaluation appear complete.

Optional analysis, dashboards, extra reviewers and broader matrices become
dependencies only when the claim requires their evidence. Prefer the supported
existing path that satisfies the agreed boundary; choosing a different route
still requires agreement when the current route is blocked. Continue useful
independent work and already authorized bounded repairs. Reassessment is not
a reason to freeze every setup task or request the same approval again.

## Results and closeout

Lead with the usable outcome: what case or suite completed through the whole
path, what its evidence supports, what remains unfinished and what specifically
blocks it. Show the next necessary action or why work should stop. Report
component checks separately; their totals cannot substitute for that outcome.

Report enough context to understand and reproduce the claim:

- Cases and attempts; acceptances, rejections, errors and incomplete trials;
  planned and valid scored trials, exclusions and metric denominators;
  reward dimensions or other evidence.
- Agent, model, effort and relevant configuration for each model-backed role;
  environment and verifier boundaries; relevant version/configuration differences.
- The effective task and context and their provenance, plus verifier and
  environment provenance; applicable usage bounds; duration and usage when available.
- Limitations, uncertainty and unresolved failures.

Link retained detail instead of copying it into every update. Estimated cost
is not authoritative billing, and one stochastic run does not establish reliability.

Lead updates with what was learned about the original question, the current
stage, and what the next action will resolve. Show concrete old and new
behavior on the same cases, valid failures, unresolved causes, and unchanged
outcomes. Model tables, review agreement, and test totals support this account;
they do not replace it. Separate a useful behavior change from more activity,
such as extra reading without better task results.

Close the iteration when its decision has sufficient evidence or its agreed
stopping condition is reached. State whether the problem was reproduced,
repaired, unreproduced, or remains unresolved, and why. Retain original results
and give remaining findings an explicit disposition, such as a blocker for
this claim or a separate follow-up. Do not silently extend the campaign or
resume deferred product work to clear unrelated findings.
