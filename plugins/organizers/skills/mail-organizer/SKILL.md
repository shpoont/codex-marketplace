# Mail Organizer

Read both bundled documents completely before processing mail:

- [Mail Processing v1.2.0](references/mail-processing.md).
- [A Simple Method for Processing Unorganized Items v1.4.0](references/simple-method-for-processing-unorganized-items.md).

Apply these fixed versions together; the shared method governs. Do not replace
them with live upstream text during a run.

Use supported capabilities and native terms. Clarify ambiguous destinations or
Inbox effects; assigning a marker alone does not authorize moving mail.

## Work interactively

Work interactively, one scope at a time, considering useful individual messages
and groups throughout. Use the purpose, known preferences and relevant context
to reduce decision effort. Clarify recurring uncertainty rather than guessing.
Prioritize outstanding needs when useful, without fixed phases or a shortlist.
Mail needing no reply is not necessarily unwanted.

When handling is undecided, present one proposal or necessary question. Give a
recommendation and reason, identifiable messages, useful sender details, key
substance, material dates, amounts or deadlines, effects including read state,
conditions and exceptions. Keep confirmations brief and the decision easy to find.
Distinguish unreviewed mail, deferred decisions and follow-up waiting elsewhere.
Make partial coverage explicit; qualify counts. End with the needed decision,
then wait. Explain actual blockers or material constraints.

Resolve the current decision or agree its deferral before moving on. Individual
discussion, task drafting and mixed handling fit within the same scope; no mode
switch is needed. Reassess when new information changes the recommendation.
Interpret advice, draft-only intent and execution authority in context. Clear
revised handling instructions can supply approval; revision alone does not require
another confirmation. Approval applies to the agreed handling; "continue" alone requests
the next proposal.

Store context once; patch only changed fields; append outcomes without recopying
history. Apply and check final handling; report verified results and unfinished
work. When work can continue, include the next proposal in the same turn without
another "next" prompt or group/individual mode switch. Ask about continuation only
to settle or extend the work boundary. Keep deferred work accessible; stop when asked.

For external handling, distinguish a blocked route from an unavailable result.
Consider permitted alternatives, reassess substantial increases in effort, and
resolve uncertainty about materially broader effects or authority before proceeding.
